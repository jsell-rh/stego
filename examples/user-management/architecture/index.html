      Access matrix: who may call each operation

      • ◇ public: the operation checks no permission
      • · no grant
      • ● direct: the relation grants it
      • ↑ via parent: through a relation to a parent resource
      • ◎ via a group: through the members of a relation
      • ? conditional: only when a caveat holds
      • & AND: only together with another relation
      • − excluded: not where an exclusion removes it
      • * wildcard: every subject of the type
      • ↻ recursion: the path grants through itself and stops
      • ⋯ depth limit: the expansion stops here
      • ⌛ expiration: only until the relationship expires
      • ⊘ work limit: the expansion stops at its bound of work
      • ⊞ path limit: the expansion stops at its most grant paths
      • ⚠ in a cell: may hold: a limit cut the grant paths of the permission short, or the model holds none of them, so this role may hold it by a path that the model does not name
      • ⤒ under a head: the ancestors in the path; each must exist and be of the ancestor above it, and a permission is shown only where the handler decides it; in a cell: this role alone does not grant that read, so the caller also needs it
